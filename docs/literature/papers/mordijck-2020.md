## Why This Study Matters | 为什么选择这项研究？

**EN** Angioni 2007 organised global density-peaking correlations; Greenwald 2007 added a discriminating device comparison. This paper advances Q001 from a correlation towards source-aware transport analysis: matched dimensionless scans, density modulation, and multiscale fluctuation measurements constrain how local density gradients change. Its value includes a counterintuitive result: stronger peaking can coexist with stronger outward diffusion. Source contribution, convective-to-diffusive ratio, perturbative stiffness and the coupled plasma state must be distinguished. This is a bounded mechanism investigation, not a complete reactor-profile prediction.

**中文** Angioni 2007 整理全局密度峰化的经验关联，Greenwald 2007 增加有判别力的装置比较。本研究通过近似匹配的无量纲扫描、密度调制和多尺度涨落测量，将 Q001 推向考虑粒子源的输运分析。其价值还包括一个重要结果：更强峰化可以与更强向外扩散同时出现。源贡献、对流与扩散之比、扰动刚性及耦合的等离子体状态须分别处理。这是有条件的机制研究，尚未完成反应堆剖面预测。

## 2. Scientific Question | 科学问题

**EN** Does the empirical peaking trend arise from beam fuelling, changes in diagonal diffusion, off-diagonal transport or a different turbulent state? Can a small density perturbation identify the steady-state flux closure, and do simulations using measured gradients explain rather than independently predict the observed profile? The paper compares three experimental operating points. It does not isolate every coupled variable or establish independent electron-/ion-gradient equilibrium sensitivities.

**中文** 峰化趋势来自束供粒、对角扩散、非对角输运还是不同的湍流状态？小密度扰动能否识别稳态通量闭合？以实测梯度为输入的模拟，是解释已有剖面还是独立预测剖面？本研究比较三个运行点，并未隔离所有耦合量，也未建立电子、离子温度梯度的独立平衡偏导响应。

**EN** Now prime means ∂/∂ρ, V′=∂V/∂ρ, A_s and φ_s are source amplitude and phase; V denotes plasma volume, not convection velocity. A has density units and A_s density/time. **EN** This separates an inward transport ratio and the source-required flux contribution; both can change. ## 5. Study Approach and Analytical Checks | 研读方法与解析核对

**EN** All 24 pages were parsed; figure/table/formula pages were visually inspected. Source identity, phase/source assumptions, signs, units, conditioning and cross-paper definitions were checked. The following are **FRA derivations under explicit assumptions**, not equations claimed verbatim from this paper. No raw-shot fitting or numerical transport calculation was performed.

**中文** 24 页均已解析阅读，图表与方程所在页已视觉核对；检查身份、幅相与源假设、符号、单位、条件数和跨论文定义。以下是**明示假设下的 FRA 推导**，不冒充论文原始方程；未拟合原始放电数据或开展数值输运计算。

### Conservation and the hybrid pinch | 守恒与混合箍缩

**ASSUMPTIONS / 假设。** Fix the equilibrium geometry, dimensionless coordinate ρ and metrics J=V′>0, G₂=〈(∇ρ)²〉>0, G₁=〈∇ρ〉>0 away from the axis. Let n>0, D>0 and v be a local diffusion–convection representation. S is a net volumetric source. / 固定几何、坐标及磁轴外正几何量，采用局部扩散—对流表示，S 为单位体积净源。

**DERIVED EQUATION / 推导方程。** Writing the radial-coordinate flux as F_ρ=−DG₂∂_ρn+vG₁n gives

$$
\partial_tn+J^{-1}\partial_\rho(JF_\rho)=S.
$$

For a stationary profile with zero inner integrated flux / 稳态且内侧积分通量为零时

$$
JF_\rho=\int_0^\rho J\bar S\,d\rho,\qquad
v=\frac{G_2}{G_1}\frac{\bar n'}{\bar n}D+
\frac{\int_0^\rho J\bar S\,d\rho}{J G_1\bar n}.
$$

**STRICT RESULT / 严格结论。** Substitution verifies this identity. It gives source Eq. (5) only after choosing D=D_p. The same observed profile and source permit many positive D and corresponding v, so Eq. (5) alone is not a second independent measurement of transport. A finite inner flux adds its integration constant; transients require the accumulation term. Near the axis the J=0 limit requires regularity. / 代入即可验证；选择 D=D_p 才得到原式（5）。同一剖面与源允许多种正 D 及相应 v，该式不是第二个独立输运测量。非零内边界通量需积分常数，时变需储积项，磁轴需另行处理正则性。

### Why phase slope matters | 相位斜率为何重要

**ASSUMPTIONS / 假设。** In the circular source-free perturbation region, use ñ=Re[A(r)e^(−iφ(r))e^(iωt)], smooth coefficients and regular inner flux. Integrating the harmonic continuity equation gives the flux amplitude F̃=−ω(Y+iX)/r. The closure F̃=−D_pN′+v_pN with N=Ae^(−iφ) then implies / 在圆形、无扰动源区域明示谐波约定，积分守恒方程并采用扰动闭合，得到

$$
\operatorname{Im}(\tilde F/N)=D_p\phi'
=-\frac{\omega(Y\sin\phi+X\cos\phi)}{rA}.
$$

**STRICT RESULT / 严格结论。** This recovers Eq. (1), and its real part recovers Eq. (2). Local coefficient identification needs nonzero Aφ′: phase and amplitude must provide independent information. At φ′=0 one cannot infer a finite unique D from this division; near zero its uncertainty is amplified. The general geometry does not remove that rank loss. This is a structural condition, not a calculated confidence interval for the shots. / 实部同样恢复式（2）。局部辨识须 Aφ′ 非零；相位斜率为零时不能据该除法识别唯一有限 D，接近零时误差放大。广义几何不消除秩退化。此为结构条件，并非已计算放电置信区间。

### Tangent coefficient versus steady coefficient | 切线系数与稳态系数

**ASSUMPTIONS / 假设。** In a fixed physical radial chart let g=∂_rn and Γ(n,g,z)=−D(n,g,z)g+nV(n,g,z), with C¹ coefficients, n>0, D>0 and internal state z. A complete first variation is / 固定物理径向坐标，令输运表示光滑，完整一阶变化为

$$
\delta\Gamma=-(D+gD_g-nV_g)\delta g+
(V+nV_n-gD_n)\delta n+(-gD_z+nV_z)\delta z.
$$

**STRICT RESULT / 严格结论。** This follows from the product/chain rules. The effective gradient response equals D+gD_g−nV_g, not always D. Source Eq. (8) additionally neglects V_g and holds n,z fixed in that derivative; real modulation changes n and may change z, so an effective fitted v can absorb other responses. Quiet DBS amplitude alone does not prove δz=0. / 由乘积及链式法则直接得到；梯度响应一般不是 D。原式（8）还忽略 V_g，并在该导数中固定 n,z；实际调制改变 n，也可能改变内部状态，拟合有效对流可吸收其他响应。DBS 幅值安静不能证明 δz=0。

Let H(g)=D(g)g on a fixed state path, so D_p=H′(g). Integration gives / 固定状态路径下积分得

$$
D(g)=\frac{H(0)+\int_0^gD_p(s)\,ds}{g},\quad g\ne0.
$$

**NECESSARY INPUT / 必需输入。** Eq. (9) sets H(0)=0, justified if D remains finite there, and needs D_p over the intervening path. Its g=0 value needs a smooth limit. The paper does not give sufficient continuous gradient-path observations or implementation details to reproduce the reported factor-three conversion independently. / 式（9）令 H(0)=0，需要 D 在零梯度处有限，还需整条中间路径上的 D_p；g=0 要取光滑极限。原文没有给足连续路径观测或实现细节，无法独立复现三倍换算。

**COUNTEREXAMPLE / 反例。** Knowing D_p only near g₀≠0 does not fix D(g₀), even with H(0)=0. Add a small smooth perturbation to D_p supported strictly between 0 and g₀, with nonzero integral. The measured neighbourhood is unchanged, while H(g₀)/g₀ changes. Positivity can be preserved by sufficiently small perturbation. Equivalently D(g)=D₀+αg² gives D_p=D₀+3αg² (D₀>0, α≥0), an analytical example of unequal steady and tangent coefficients; it is not a fitted physical model. / 只知道非零 g₀ 邻域的 D_p，仍无法确定稳态 D。两端之间加小的光滑扰动，保留所测邻域却改变积分，且可保正。简单光滑例子也表明稳态与切线系数不同；不将其作为拟合物理模型。

**STIFFNESS AUDIT / 刚性审计。** With V_g=0, the diffusion-part stiffness is κ_D=gH′/H=D_p/D. The unnumbered expression on PDF p.14 equates denominators Γ−vn and Γ after setting the derivative of vn to zero. That derivative assumption alone does not remove a nonzero flux offset. The total-flux normalisation differs and can be singular at Γ=0. Thus use the reported coefficient ratio conditionally; do not turn the printed second equality into an unrestricted identity. / 对流无梯度导数时，扩散部分刚性为 D_p/D；原文从导数为零进一步替换非零通量偏置的分母，不是一般恒等式。总通量归一化不同，并可在 Γ=0 奇异。

## 7. Limitations & Open Questions | 局限与开放问题

**EN** The study supplies more constraints than a stationary correlation: source-aware modulation and independent density diagnostics. Its strongest mechanism attribution remains conditional on local transport, source approximation, fitted phase derivatives and the PB reconstruction. The smallest collisionality case changes ion gradients, E_r and fast-ion fraction, so a direct collision-only interpretation is not identified. ## 8. Contribution to Fusion Research | 对聚变研究的贡献

**EN** Q001 gains a physical distinction: peaking depends on a flux balance and ratios of transport coefficients, not on source strength or diffusivity in isolation. **中文** Q001 得到的推进是：峰化由通量平衡与输运比共同决定，不能单看源强或扩散。研究也说明切线输运响应与稳态归因需要分别取证。模拟有助于解释实测状态；未来装置预测仍需源一致的、经检验的闭合及未见剖面上的验证，反应堆收益仍属有条件前景。

## 9. Cross-disciplinary Research Opportunities | 跨学科研究机会

**EN** Treat gas modulation as an inverse problem with geometry, source and measurement operators, not as two directly observed coefficients. Propagate amplitude/phase covariance and compare admissible coefficient families. Model-selection tasks must distinguish a conditioned gradient-driven explanation from a prospective flux-driven prediction. Diagnostic forward models should compare fluctuation spectra and cross-phases at the measured wavenumbers, rather than equate a linear eigenvalue with a measured flux.

**中文** 将调制表述为包含几何、源与测量算子的反问题，而不是两个直接观测系数；传播幅相协方差，比较容许系数族。模型比较区分给定梯度的条件解释与前瞻通量驱动预测，诊断前向模型应在实测波数上比较谱和交叉相位，不将线性本征值等同于实测通量。
