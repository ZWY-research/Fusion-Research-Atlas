## 2. Scientific Question | 核心科学问题

**EN** How does density peaking scale across the combined AUG/JET H-mode observations, including the roles of collisionality and the NBI particle source? This is an organising question based on the title and saved abstract-level record, not a full-text reconstruction of the authors' argument. It is relevant to the existing q-density question, which asks how particle sources, diffusion, and convection contribute to density profiles.

**中文** 在 AUG/JET 联合 H 模观测中，密度峰化呈现怎样的标度，碰撞性与 NBI 粒子源起什么作用？这是根据标题和已存摘要级记录形成的组织性问题，并非对作者全文论证的重构。它与既有 q-density 问题相关：粒子源、扩散和对流如何共同影响密度剖面？
