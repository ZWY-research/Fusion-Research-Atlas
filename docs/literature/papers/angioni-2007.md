**EN — Reading result.** The study offers a carefully harmonised, two-device empirical model of **global density peaking**. Collisionality is the most relevant fitted variable in its tested models, but the data do not eliminate Greenwald-based alternatives. Beam fuelling contributes under a transport-ratio assumption and cannot account for the full variation within those models. ## 2. Scientific Question | 核心科学问题

**EN** At fixed volume-averaged density and temperature profiles, density-profile shape affects fusion power and bootstrap current; excessive peaking can also complicate MHD stability and impurity accumulation. These are the authors' motivations, not performance gains measured in this study. The empirical question is whether collisionality, proximity to the Greenwald density limit, beam fuelling or device-related differences best organise H-mode peaking, and how that choice changes an ITER prediction. **Source: §1, p. 2.**

**中文** 在体积平均密度与温度剖面固定的条件下，密度形状会影响聚变功率及自举电流；过强峰化也可能带来 MHD 稳定性与杂质积累问题。这些属于作者研究动机，不是本文实测的性能增益。经验问题是：碰撞性、距 Greenwald 密度极限的程度、束供粒及装置相关差异，哪些最能组织 H 模峰化变化？变量选择又如何改变 ITER 预测？**来源：第 1 节，第 2 页。**

**EN** A single device cannot cleanly discriminate these explanations because its collisionality covaries with Greenwald fraction, normalised gyroradius and beam fuelling. Adding a different-sized device can reduce some covariances. Adding the dimensional major radius to otherwise dimensionless regressors then tests for a residual device offset. This is a useful consistency diagnostic, not a theorem that every major-radius coefficient is unphysical. ITER's low collisionality and relatively high Greenwald fraction motivate comparing models whose extrapolations point in different directions. **Source: §§1, 3, pp. 2–3.**

**中文** 单装置中碰撞性与 Greenwald 分数、归一化回旋半径和束供粒共同变化，难以清楚区分解释。增加不同尺寸装置可减少部分协方差；在无量纲回归变量之外加入有量纲大半径，则用于检查残留装置偏移。这是有用的一致性诊断，不是“大半径系数必然不物理”的定理。ITER 的低碰撞性与较高 Greenwald 分数，使不同方向的外推尤其值得比较。**来源：第 1、3 节，第 2–3 页。**

### Observable and radial coordinates | 观测量与径向坐标

**SOURCE DEFINITION / 原文定义 — §4, p. 6:**

$$
\mathrm{pk}_{n_e}=\frac{n_{e2}}{\langle n_e\rangle_{\rm Vol}},
\qquad n_{e2}:=n_e(\rho_{\rm pol}=0.2).
$$

**EN** The numerator is a near-core value at the stated flux coordinate, not an asserted magnetic-axis density. Angle brackets are a **volume average**, distinct from the line average entering $F_{GR}$. In integral notation this means $\langle n_e\rangle_{\rm Vol}=V_{\rm pl}^{-1}\int n_e\,dV$, with equilibrium volume weighting. The paper uses normalised poloidal-flux coordinate $\rho_{\rm pol}$ but does not supply an explicit formula mapping it to geometric $r/a$; its source/heat fluxes are evaluated separately at **$r/a=0.5$**. These two coordinates must not be identified without equilibrium information. The choice of peak-to-average ratio is driven by what five chord integrals constrain well, rather than an ability to recover an exact local profile. **Source: §§3–4, pp. 4–7.**

**中文** 分子是在给定磁通坐标处的近核心值，不能直接称为磁轴密度。尖括号表示**体积平均**，与 $F_{GR}$ 使用的线平均不同。用积分写法即 $\langle n_e\rangle_{\rm Vol}=V_{\rm pl}^{-1}\int n_e\,dV$，须包含平衡体积权重。原文使用归一化极向磁通坐标 $\rho_{\rm pol}$，但未提供它与几何 $r/a$ 的显式换算式；源项与热通量则另在 **$r/a=0.5$** 计算。没有平衡信息时不得将二者等同。选择峰值与体积平均之比，是因为五条弦积分较好地约束这一量，不是因为能够精确重建局部剖面。**来源：第 3–4 节，第 4–7 页。**

**FRA ANALYSIS / FRA 分析 — global peaking does not determine a local gradient.**

$$
g_n(r):=\frac{R}{L_{n_e}}=-R\,\frac{d\ln n_e}{dr},
\qquad
\mathrm{pk}_{n_e}\ne g_n(r).
$$

**EN** The first quantity compares two global/profile values; the second differentiates the profile at a specified radius. To see the information loss, take a smooth positive profile $n_0$ and a smooth perturbation $f$ with $f(r_2)=0$, $\int f\,dV=0$, and $f(r_m)=0$ but $f'(r_m)\ne0$, where $r_2$ and $r_m$ are distinct interior radii and the equilibrium geometry and $R$ are fixed. Such an $f$ can be built from a bump through $r_m$ plus a disjoint compensating bump. For sufficiently small $\epsilon$, $n_0+\epsilon f$ stays positive and has exactly the same peaking, yet changes $g_n(r_m)$ by $-R\epsilon f'(r_m)/n_0(r_m)$. This is a profile-level counterexample, not a claim to reproduce an AUG/JET equilibrium. A conversion requires a constrained profile family or an independently checked calibration; it cannot be inferred from the peaking fit alone.

**中文** 前者比较剖面中的值与整体平均，后者对指定半径处的剖面求导。信息损失可直接展示：固定平衡几何和 $R$，取光滑正剖面 $n_0$ 与光滑扰动 $f$，使 $f(r_2)=0$、$\int f\,dV=0$，并在另一个内部半径 $r_m$ 满足 $f(r_m)=0$、$f'(r_m)\ne0$。可用穿过 $r_m$ 的局部扰动加上不相交的补偿扰动构造这种 $f$。充分小的 $\epsilon$ 保持 $n_0+\epsilon f$ 为正，峰化因子完全不变，但 $g_n(r_m)$ 改变 $-R\epsilon f'(r_m)/n_0(r_m)$。这是剖面层面的反例，不是对 AUG/JET 平衡的复现。二者换算需要受约束剖面族或独立检查的标定，不能由峰化回归本身推出。

### Cross-device diagnostic harmonisation | 跨装置诊断协调

**EN** JET profiles are obtained by singular value decomposition inversion (SVD-I), with basis information from LIDAR Thomson scattering. The authors assume these JET reconstructions are the more reliable reference because of their agreement with interferometer integrals; they cite earlier checks rather than re-establishing them here. They map each JET profile, preserving its density-versus-flux-coordinate relation, onto one fixed AUG equilibrium, calculate synthetic integrals along the **five AUG DCN interferometer chords**, then reinvert with five chosen basis functions. The same basis/inversion procedure is used on actual AUG line integrals. Selection aims at faithful JET peaking and regular, approximately monotonic AUG profiles; the basis need not be orthogonal. **Source: §4, pp. 5–7; Figs. 1–2, pp. 19–20.**

**中文** JET 剖面采用奇异值分解反演（singular value decomposition inversion, SVD-I），基函数信息来自 LIDAR 汤姆孙散射。作者依据其与干涉仪线积分的符合程度，将 JET 重建作为较可靠参照；既有一致性检查在本文中被引用，没有重新实施。随后保持密度与磁通坐标的关系，将各 JET 剖面映射到一个固定 AUG 平衡，计算**五条 AUG DCN 干涉仪弦**的合成积分，再用五个选定基函数反演。对真实 AUG 线积分采用相同基函数与反演方法，兼顾 JET 峰化重建与 AUG 剖面规则性、近似单调性；基函数不要求正交。**来源：第 4 节，第 5–7 页；图 1–2，第 19–20 页。**

**EN** Re-inverted JET peaking differs from the original SVD-I peaking with **RMSE 0.018**. Original JET mean/SD are 1.454/0.193; the harmonised values in Table 2 are 1.457/0.188. AUG's harmonised mean/SD are approximately 1.37/0.201, versus 1.31/0.175 from its routine Abel inversion with lithium-beam edge information. The roughly 0.06 shift illustrates why diagnostic mismatch can masquerade as device-size dependence. The 0.018 figure is a conditional synthetic reconstruction check against the chosen reference profiles; it is not a total experimental error bar or an independent validation of all AUG profiles. **Source: §4, pp. 6–7; Table 2.**

**中文** JET 再反演峰化与原 SVD-I 峰化之间的 **RMSE 为 0.018**。原 JET 均值/标准差为 1.454/0.193，表 2 中协调后的值为 1.457/0.188。AUG 协调后均值/标准差约为 1.37/0.201，而常规 Abel 反演结合锂束边界信息得到 1.31/0.175。约 0.06 的偏移说明诊断差异为何会伪装成装置尺寸依赖。0.018 是在选定参考剖面下的合成重建检查，不是总实验误差条，也不是对所有 AUG 剖面的独立验证。**来源：第 4 节，第 6–7 页；表 2。**

### Particle balance and the NBI proxy | 粒子平衡与 NBI 代理量

**EN** JET uses the steady-state Fokker–Planck **PENCIL** code and AUG the Monte Carlo **FAFNER** code to calculate beam source and heating profiles, including injection geometry, beam energies, equilibria and plasma profiles. The alternative source-shape regressor is $S_{NBI}(\rho_{\rm pol}=0.2)/\langle S_{NBI}\rangle_{\rm Vol}$; $S_{NBI}$ is electrons produced per volume and time through beam ionisation/charge exchange. The preferred proxy combines source flux and power balance. Wall-neutral supply in the confinement region is neglected by reference to prior work, not measured to be zero here. **Source: §3, pp. 3–5; §5, p. 7.**

**中文** JET 用稳态 Fokker–Planck **PENCIL**，AUG 用蒙特卡洛 **FAFNER** 计算束粒子源与加热剖面，输入包括注入几何、束能量、平衡及等离子体剖面。另一候选变量是源形状 $S_{NBI}(\rho_{\rm pol}=0.2)/\langle S_{NBI}\rangle_{\rm Vol}$；$S_{NBI}$ 表示束电离/电荷交换产生的单位体积、单位时间电子源。优选代理量将源通量与功率平衡结合。约束区壁中性粒子供给因既有研究而被忽略，并非本文实测为零。**来源：第 3 节，第 3–5 页；第 5 节，第 7 页。**

**SOURCE EQUATION (1) / 原文式 (1) — p. 4:**

$$
-\frac{1}{n}\frac{dn}{dr}=\frac{\Gamma}{nD}-\frac{V}{D}.
$$

**EN** This rewrites $\Gamma=-D\,dn/dr+Vn$ for a local steady diffusion–convection representation. With outward-positive flux, $D>0$ and $r$ increasing outward, an inward convective velocity $V<0$ makes $-V/D$ positive. $n$ is density, $\Gamma$ particle flux, $D$ particle diffusivity and $V$ convection; units are $\mathrm{m}^{-3}$, $\mathrm{m}^{-2}\mathrm{s}^{-1}$, $\mathrm{m}^2\mathrm{s}^{-1}$ and $\mathrm{m}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$. The equation separates source-required flux from convection for a given profile; it does not establish unique, separately measured $D$ and $V$, or require zero particle flux. Its total $\Gamma$ must be distinguished from the beam-only $\Gamma_{NBI}$.

**中文** 该式是局部稳态扩散–对流表示 $\Gamma=-D\,dn/dr+Vn$ 的改写。采用向外为正、$D>0$、$r$ 向外增加时，向内对流 $V<0$ 使 $-V/D$ 为正。$n,\Gamma,D,V$ 分别是密度、粒子通量、粒子扩散系数和对流速度，单位分别为 $\mathrm{m}^{-3}$、$\mathrm{m}^{-2}\mathrm{s}^{-1}$、$\mathrm{m}^2\mathrm{s}^{-1}$、$\mathrm{m}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$。该表示区分给定剖面下源项所要求的通量与对流贡献，却没有给出唯一、分别实测的 $D,V$，也不要求零粒子通量。总 $\Gamma$ 需与仅由束产生的 $\Gamma_{NBI}$ 区分。

**SOURCE EQUATION (2) / 原文式 (2) — p. 4:**

$$
\Gamma^{\ast}_{NBI}:=\frac{R\Gamma_{NBI}}{nD}\frac{D}{\chi}
=2T\frac{\Gamma_{NBI}}{Q_{NBI}}\frac{Q_{NBI}}{Q_{TOT}}
\left|\frac{R}{T}\frac{dT}{dr}\right|.
$$

**EN** $Q_{NBI}$ and $Q_{TOT}$ are beam and total heat fluxes; $T$ is temperature in energy units consistent with those fluxes, and $\chi=(\chi_i+\chi_e)/2$ is the mean heat diffusivity under the approximation of no large electron–ion temperature difference. Algebraically, $D$ cancels from the **proxy definition**, giving $\Gamma^{\ast}_{NBI}=R\Gamma_{NBI}/(n\chi)$; the unknown source contribution to $R/L_n$ remains $(\chi/D)\Gamma^{\ast}_{NBI}$. Thus the construction avoids measuring $D$ but does not remove the transport-ratio assumption from the interpretation. The strongest assumption is that $\chi/D$ is approximately constant across the dataset; its average is fitted, not fixed to a chosen value before regression. All fluxes are computed at $r/a=0.5$, with coupled RF power assumed absorbed inside that radius. **Source: §3, pp. 4–5.**

**中文** $Q_{NBI},Q_{TOT}$ 分别为束热通量与总热通量；$T$ 使用与热通量一致的能量单位，$\chi=(\chi_i+\chi_e)/2$ 是在电子、离子温度差不大的近似下采用的平均热扩散系数。$D$ 在**代理量定义**中代数消去，得到 $\Gamma^{\ast}_{NBI}=R\Gamma_{NBI}/(n\chi)$；对 $R/L_n$ 的未知源贡献仍是 $(\chi/D)\Gamma^{\ast}_{NBI}$。因此，这一构造避免直接测量 $D$，但没有消除解释所需的输运比假设。最强假设是 $\chi/D$ 在全数据库近似恒定；其平均值由拟合确定，不是回归前人为指定。全部通量在 $r/a=0.5$ 计算，且假设耦合 RF 功率在该半径内吸收。**来源：第 3 节，第 4–5 页。**

**EN — Sign and zero-source care.** The prose below Eq. (2) writes $\chi=Q_{TOT}/(2n\,dT/dr)$ without a minus sign, whereas Eq. (2) uses an absolute gradient. For an outward-positive heat flux and decreasing temperature, a positive diffusivity uses $|dT/dr|$ in this magnitude relation. This review preserves Eq. (2) and records the prose ambiguity instead of silently attributing a signed convention to the source. Also, $\Gamma_{NBI}/Q_{NBI}$ is not evaluated as $0/0$ in an RF-only discharge: use the reduced proxy $R\Gamma_{NBI}/(n\chi)$ with finite $\chi$, which has zero beam contribution. No zero-total-flux inference follows.

**中文 — 符号与零源处理。** 式 (2) 后正文写 $\chi=Q_{TOT}/(2n\,dT/dr)$，没有负号，而式 (2) 使用梯度绝对值。若热通量向外为正且温度向外下降，正热扩散系数的幅值关系应使用 $|dT/dr|$。本页保留式 (2)，登记正文符号含混，不擅自替作者指定有符号约定。另外，仅 RF 放电不能将 $\Gamma_{NBI}/Q_{NBI}$ 直接计算为 $0/0$；有限 $\chi$ 下使用约简代理量 $R\Gamma_{NBI}/(n\chi)$，束贡献为零，但不能推出总粒子通量为零。

**SOURCE CALIBRATION / 原文标定 — §3, pp. 4–5:**

$$
\frac{R}{L_T}=3.23\left(\frac{T_2}{\langle T\rangle}-0.37\right),
\qquad T_2=T(\rho_{\rm pol}=0.2).
$$

**EN** The relation comes from **150 AUG and 200 JET** well-diagnosed temperature profiles; mean-normalised RMSE is **9.72%**. For the peaking regression the authors replace the gradient in Eq. (2) by $(T_2/\langle T\rangle-0.37)$. This changes normalisation; recovering $\chi/D$ from a global-peaking coefficient additionally requires the conversion between density peaking and the local density gradient. The authors report average ratios near 1.5 for collisionality models and near 2.5 for Greenwald-only alternatives (§6, p. 10); this manuscript does not provide the full numerical density-gradient conversion needed to independently reproduce that renormalisation. Neither number is a direct turbulence-coefficient measurement.

**中文** 标定采用 **150 个 AUG、200 个 JET** 诊断较好的温度剖面，归一到均值的 RMSE 为 **9.72%**。峰化回归中，作者用 $(T_2/\langle T\rangle-0.37)$ 替代式 (2) 的梯度，这会改变归一化。从全局峰化回归系数恢复 $\chi/D$，还需密度峰化与局部密度梯度之间的换算。作者在第 6 节第 10 页报告碰撞性模型的平均比值约 1.5、Greenwald 竞争模型约 2.5；本文稿未给出足以独立重做该重归一化的完整密度梯度换算数值。这两个数都不是湍流输运系数的直接测量。

### Statistical method and what it identifies | 统计方法与可识别内容

**SOURCE DEFINITIONS / 原文定义 — §6, p. 8:**

$$
Y=c+\sum_j\widehat a_jX_j,
\qquad \mathrm{StR}_j=\widehat a_j\,\mathrm{STD}(X_j),
\qquad \mathrm{StS}_j=\frac{\widehat a_j}{\mathrm{STD}(a_j)}.
$$

**EN** Fits use robust **iteratively reweighted least squares with bisquare weighting**. The paper also considers logarithmic-response fits but reports linear-response fits because the additive source/pinch structure of Eq. (1) motivates them. In the reported model, $Y=\mathrm{pk}_{n_e}$ and one regressor is $X=\ln\nu_{\rm eff}$: “linear regression” does not mean linearity in raw collisionality. StR is a signed, one-regressor-standard-deviation fitted effect with other model regressors fixed; compare magnitudes for relevance. StS divides the coefficient by its estimated standard deviation. Neither is a physical partial derivative or a causal effect. Variable elimination is iterative, removing the least significant variable until excluding another substantially worsens RMSE; the author's benchmark is $\Delta\mathrm{RMSE}$ relative to $\mathrm{RMSE}/N$. **Source: §6, p. 8; §7, p. 12.**

**中文** 拟合使用带双平方权重的**迭代重加权最小二乘**。作者同时考察对数响应拟合，但主要报告线性响应形式，因为式 (1) 中源与箍缩的可加结构为此提供动机。报告模型的 $Y=\mathrm{pk}_{n_e}$，其中一个变量是 $X=\ln\nu_{\rm eff}$；“线性回归”并不表示对原始碰撞性线性。StR 是其他模型变量固定时，一个自变量标准差对应的有符号拟合变化，比较关联强度应看其绝对值。StS 是系数除以其估计标准差。二者都不是物理偏导数或因果效应。变量采用逐次剔除法：删除最不显著变量，直到继续删除明显增大 RMSE；作者比较的是 $\Delta\mathrm{RMSE}$ 相对 $\mathrm{RMSE}/N$。**来源：第 6 节，第 8 页；第 7 节，第 12 页。**

**EN** Reported coefficient uncertainties are labelled by the authors as one standard deviation and “66.7% confidence intervals”. This review retains the numerical uncertainties as reported; it does not convert them to 95% coverage or certify the sampling assumptions. No raw-data reweighting, shot-cluster uncertainty estimate or held-out-device validation was performed by FRA.

**中文** 作者将系数不确定性标为一个标准差及“66.7% 置信区间”。本页按原数值登记，不将其转换成 95% 覆盖率，也不认证其抽样假设。FRA 没有重做原始数据加权、按放电聚类的不确定性估计或留出装置验证。

**FRA ANALYSIS — shared measurement dependence / FRA 分析：共同测量依赖。**

**EN** Peaking contains $\langle n_e\rangle$ in its denominator, while $\nu_{\rm eff}$ contains the same measured quantity in its numerator. For an illustrative small fractional error $\eta$ in the mean density alone, with $n_{e2}$, temperature and geometry held fixed, $\delta\ln\mathrm{pk}_{n_e}=-\eta$ and $\delta\ln\nu_{\rm eff}=\eta$ to first order. This error component contributes negative covariance $-\mathrm{Var}(\eta)$. A uniform multiplicative calibration error affecting both numerator and denominator of peaking would instead cancel from their ratio. Thus correlated profile errors must be propagated jointly; this example identifies a possible coupling, not evidence that the reported −0.76 correlation is an artefact. The required covariance is unavailable for quantifying it here.

**中文** 峰化分母含 $\langle n_e\rangle$，碰撞性分子又使用同一测量量。作为小误差例子，若仅平均密度有相对误差 $\eta$，同时固定 $n_{e2}$、温度与几何，则一阶有 $\delta\ln\mathrm{pk}_{n_e}=-\eta$、$\delta\ln\nu_{\rm eff}=\eta$，该误差分量贡献负协方差 $-\mathrm{Var}(\eta)$。若统一乘性校准误差同时作用于峰化的分子和分母，则会在比值中消去。因此剖面相关误差需联合传播；这一例子只识别可能耦合，不证明已报告的 −0.76 相关是伪效应。本页缺少定量评估所需协方差。

**EN** In Fig. 3, peaking has correlation −0.76 with collisionality, while the source proxy has correlation 0.52 with peaking and −0.37 with collisionality in the full sample. ### What the NBI percentage means | NBI 百分比的含义

**EN** The source attribution is not “30% of all particles come from NBI” or a measured causal variance fraction. It describes the estimated contribution to the peaking variation under the fitted transport representation. As an **FRA arithmetic check**, Eq. (3)'s coefficient 1.331 and Table 2's source SD 0.044 imply a one-SD fitted change $1.331\times0.044=0.0586$; compared with peaking SD 0.200, this is about 29.3%. Eq. (4) gives $1.911\times0.044/0.200\simeq42.0\%$. These scales are consistent with the author's roughly 30%/40% descriptions, but do not supply an independent attribution or certify a universal upper bound. Covariant terms cannot have their absolute fitted variations summed as uniquely identifiable physical contributions. **Source: §6, pp. 9–10; Eqs. (3)–(4); Table 2.**

**中文** 源项归因不是“NBI 提供全部粒子的 30%”，也不是实测的因果方差份额。它描述拟合输运表示下对峰化变化的估计贡献。作为 **FRA 的算术核对**，式 (3) 系数 1.331 乘以表 2 源项标准差 0.044，得到一标准差拟合变化 $0.0586$；除以峰化标准差 0.200，约为 29.3%。式 (4) 则给出 $1.911\times0.044/0.200\simeq42.0\%$。这些量级与作者约 30%/40% 的表述一致，却不构成独立归因或普适上界证明。具有协方差的各项绝对拟合变化不能相加后解释为唯一可识别的物理贡献。**来源：第 6 节，第 9–10 页；式 (3)–(4)；表 2。**

**EN** NBI produces finite outward source-required flux, diffusion tends to flatten a decreasing density profile, and inward convection helps sustain peaking. Eq. (1) contains all three roles, but stationary data cannot uniquely split diffusivity and convection: for a known positive profile $n$ and flux $\Gamma$, **any admissible $D(r)>0$** can be paired with $V(r)=\Gamma(r)/n(r)+D(r)n'(r)/n(r)$ to satisfy the same balance. This exact algebraic family explains why a transport-model assumption or transient measurement is needed. The paper's evidence rejects a beam-only explanation within its analysed models; it does not distinguish all possible source-dependent diffusivity and pinch structures.

**中文** NBI 产生有限的、由源所要求的向外粒子通量；扩散倾向于展平向外下降的密度剖面，向内对流帮助维持峰化。式 (1) 包含三种作用，但稳态数据不能唯一拆分扩散与对流：给定正剖面 $n$ 和通量 $\Gamma$，**任意可容许的 $D(r)>0$** 都可配上 $V(r)=\Gamma(r)/n(r)+D(r)n'(r)/n(r)$，满足同一平衡。这一精确代数族说明为何需要输运模型假设或瞬态测量。论文证据在所分析模型内排除了仅束供粒解释，却没有区分所有源依赖扩散与箍缩结构。

**EN — Direct scientific contribution.** The work advances Q001 by showing how much more can be learned from a diagnostic-consistent joint dataset than from device-specific correlations: collisionality remains relevant after including source and geometry variables, while the weak dimensional-size role provides an additional consistency argument. This paper gives a practical warning: adopting one fit solely because it has low RMSE can hide a materially different future profile. A source-normalised comparison and RF-only checks can guide future measurements; they are not a demonstrated control strategy, validated reactor performance improvement or universal transport law.

**中文 — 潜在物理或工程影响。** 密度剖面模型可用于运行方案估计，但须分别报告源/箍缩假设、诊断不确定性和竞争外推。本文给出实际警示：仅因 RMSE 较低就采用一个拟合，会隐藏未来剖面的实质性差异。源归一化比较与仅 RF 检查可指导后续测量，但还不是已验证控制策略、反应堆性能改进或普适输运规律。

**EN — Long-term fusion link.** Density shape enters confinement, stability and burning-plasma performance considerations. Moving from this empirical evidence to credible reactor prediction requires measured transport closure or validated physics models, source balance including the relevant reactor fuelling, and tests of transfer between devices and regimes. The paper contributes an observational constraint and competing forecasts to that chain, not proof of the completed chain. **Source: §§1, 7–8.**

**中文 — 长期聚变目标的联系。** 密度形状进入约束、稳定性和燃烧等离子体性能问题。从这些经验依据走向可信反应堆预测，还需实测输运闭合或验证过的物理模型、包含相应反应堆供粒的源平衡，以及跨装置、跨运行条件迁移检验。本文向这条链条提供观测约束与竞争预测，没有证明整条链条已闭合。**来源：第 1、7–8 节。**

## 9. Cross-disciplinary Research Opportunities | 跨学科研究机会

**EN — Inverse problems.** Treat the interferometer as a line-integral operator on positive density profiles, not a direct local-density sensor. With known equilibrium, chords, uncertainties and independent profile information, seek bounds on $n_e(\rho_{\rm pol}=0.2)/\langle n_e\rangle$ and $R/L_n$ over profiles consistent with the measurements. Compare bounds with and without the five-function restriction; carry the admissible interval into cross-device regressions. The return to plasma physics is whether a claimed parameter trend survives permissible diagnostic/profile changes. **Basis: A07-C02 and the global/local counterexample.**

**中文 — 反问题。** 将干涉仪表述为正密度剖面上的线积分算子，而非局部密度传感器。给定平衡、弦、误差及独立剖面信息，在符合测量的剖面集合上求峰化与 $R/L_n$ 的界，比较有无五基函数约束的差异，并将可容许区间传入跨装置回归。返回等离子体物理的检验是：参数趋势能否在允许的诊断和剖面变化下保持。**依据：A07-C02 及全局/局部反例。**

**EN — Transport identifiability.** Eq. (1)'s stationary $D,V$ family is a concrete non-identifiability problem. Time-resolved density response to a calibrated source perturbation, together with power balance, may constrain the family under a specified local diffusion–convection approximation. Test whether inferred $\chi/D$ is sufficiently stable to justify using one global source coefficient, and whether its variation instead accounts for a fitted collisionality trend. The task requires temporal resolution, perturbation amplitude control and explicit validity of the local representation; fitting a stationary profile again cannot resolve it. **Basis: Eq. (1), A07-C06, A07-C08.**

**中文 — 输运可识别性。** 式 (1) 的稳态 $D,V$ 族提供一个具体不可识别问题。在明确局部扩散–对流近似下，用标定源扰动的时间分辨密度响应，结合功率平衡，可能约束这个族。需检验识别出的 $\chi/D$ 是否足够稳定，支持一个全局源系数；或其变化能否解释拟合中的碰撞性趋势。任务要求时间分辨率、扰动幅度控制和局部表示的有效性，重复拟合一个稳态剖面不能解决它。**依据：式 (1)、A07-C06、A07-C08。**

**EN — Statistical transportability.** Distinguish a within-device conditional association, a device-level calibration offset, and a model that predicts new operating regimes. With authorised observation-level data, measurement covariance, shot labels, heating composition and electron/ion temperature ratios, use shot-grouped uncertainty resampling and predeclared device/regime holdouts. Keep the source construction and diagnostic model fixed when comparing collisionality and Greenwald candidates. Candidate performance must then be tested in plasma regimes that separate the competing covariates; a resampled regression alone does not create causal control. **Basis: A07-C03–C05, A07-C07–C08.**

**中文 — 统计迁移能力。** 区分装置内条件关联、装置级校准偏移，以及能预测新运行区间的模型。获得授权的逐观测数据、测量协方差、放电标签、加热构成和电子/离子温度比后，可采用按放电分组的不确定性重采样及预先指定的装置/条件留出。比较碰撞性与 Greenwald 模型时保持源构造和诊断模型一致，再在能够分开竞争变量的等离子体条件下检验预测；仅重采样回归不能创造因果控制。**依据：A07-C03–C05、A07-C07–C08。**
