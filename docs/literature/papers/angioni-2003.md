## Why This Study Matters | 为什么选择这项研究？

**EN** The paper [@paper-angioni-2003] moves Q001 beyond the statement that density is peaked: it combines an AUG peaking/collisionality association with a discriminating comparison of transport closures and a collision switch-off inside GLF23. ## 2. Scientific Question | 核心科学问题

**EN — Authors' question.** Why do some experiments require anomalous inward particle transport beyond the neoclassical Ware pinch, while others appear consistent with a pinch of Ware magnitude? The authors ask whether collisional effects on ITG/TEM transport can reconcile those observations (p.1 introduction; p.4 conclusions). Their abstract claims a first clear experimental demonstration; a priority claim over all preceding literature is not independently assessed here.

**中文——作者问题。** 为什么部分实验需要超过新经典 Ware 箍缩的反常内向输运，另一些却似乎只需 Ware 量级？作者研究碰撞对 ITG/TEM 输运的影响能否协调这些观测（第 1 页引言、第 4 页结论）。摘要自称首次给出清晰实验证据；本次未对所有前序文献独立进行优先权评价。

**EN — FRA reformulation.** Separate (i) the measured peaking/collisionality association, (ii) the effect of a controlled change inside a declared transport model, and (iii) identification of the actual microscopic carrier of particle flux. A successful model intervention can strengthen a mechanism argument without becoming an experimental isolation of collisionality.

**中文——FRA 重述。** 区分三层问题：峰化与碰撞性的实测关联、明确输运模型内部的受控改变，以及真实粒子通量微观载体的识别。模型干预成功可增强机制论证，但不等于实验独立隔离碰撞性。

## 3. Research Methods | 研究方法

### Experimental observables and coordinates | 实验观测量与坐标

**EN** Fig. 1 defines peaking as n_e(ρ_ψ=0.4)/n_e(ρ_ψ=0.8), with ρ_ψ=√(Ψ/Ψ_edge), a normalised **poloidal**-flux radius. Panel (a) fixes P_NBI=5 MW and groups q_95≈3,4,5. Panel (b) uses q_95≈4 and line-average density windows 0.5<n_el20<0.55 and 0.75<n_el20<0.8, where n_el20 is measured in 10²⁰ m⁻³; its inset instead plots peaking against that density. The text attributes within-window collisionality variation to electron-temperature changes. This narrows some alternatives but does not fix temperature, Z_eff, all gradients, equilibrium or sources independently. The Letter does not provide a complete diagnostic/error covariance pipeline.

**中文** Fig. 1 定义峰化为 n_e(ρ_ψ=0.4)/n_e(ρ_ψ=0.8)，ρ_ψ=√(Ψ/Ψ_edge) 是归一化**极向**磁通半径。(a) 固定 P_NBI=5 MW，区分 q_95≈3、4、5；(b) 取 q_95≈4，线平均密度窗口为 0.5<n_el20<0.55、0.75<n_el20<0.8，n_el20 单位为 10²⁰ m⁻³，插图则以密度为横轴。正文将窗口内碰撞性变化联系至电子温度变化。这减少部分替代解释，但并未独立固定温度、Z_eff、全部梯度、平衡与源。短文未提供完整诊断／误差协方差链。

**EN** On p.1 the dimensionless collisionality is the electron–ion collision frequency divided by the curvature-drift estimate:

**中文** 第 1 页的无量纲碰撞性为电子—离子碰撞频率与曲率漂移频率估计之比：

$$
\nu_{eff}=\frac{\nu_{ei}}{\omega_{De}},\qquad
\omega_{De}=\frac{2k_\perp\rho_s c_s}{R},\quad
k_\perp\rho_s=\sqrt{0.1},\quad
\rho_s=\frac{c_s}{\Omega_{ci}},\quad c_s=\sqrt{\frac{T_e}{m_i}},\quad
\Omega_{ci}=\frac{Z_i eB}{m_i}.
$$

**EN** R is major radius, k_perp the perpendicular wavenumber, m_i ion mass, Z_i e ion charge and T_e in energy units. This proxy is not automatically the same as trapped-electron bounce collisionality or a later paper's ν*. The printed square root in √0.1 matters to the frequency normalisation.

**中文** R 为大半径，k_perp 为垂直波数，m_i 为离子质量，Z_i e 为离子电荷，T_e 以能量单位表示。该代理量不能自动等同于俘获电子弹跳碰撞性或后续论文的 ν*；原刊 √0.1 的根号影响频率归一化。

**EN** Fig. 2 uses 30 representative cases from Fig. 1(a), 0.1≤ν_eff≤2: I_p=1 MA, B_t=2.0–2.6 T, triangularity δ<0.2, P_NBI≈5 MW, q_95≈4 and volume-average n_e=(2.9–8.6)×10¹⁹ m⁻³. Z_eff is around 2.5 at low density and below 2 at high density. **中文** Fig. 2(a) 画 ε_ne 对体平均密度，(b)、(c) 画 η_e、η_i 对 ε_ne。实验星号与含碰撞 GLF23 圆圈体现密度标长增大的趋势，无碰撞／反应式变体未恢复该趋势。图中仍有散布，本次未重建数值拟合或误差评分。Weiland 与 GLF23 比较同时改变多种假设；第四组计算更具体检验的是 **GLF23 内部**碰撞作用，而非实际等离子体全部机制。

**EN** Page 2 defines η_σ=L_nσ/L_Tσ and ε_nσ=2L_nσ/L_B, with L_nσ⁻¹=−∂_ρ ln n_σ, L_Tσ⁻¹=−∂_ρ ln T_σ and L_B⁻¹=|∂_ρ ln B|≈1/R. Here σ=e,i, **ρ=aρ_φ**, ρ_φ=√(Φ/Φ_edge) uses **toroidal** flux, and a is the outer-midplane minor radius. Fig. 2(a) averages over ρ_φ=0.4–0.7, as does Fig. 3. Neither ρ_φ nor the dimensional ρ should be substituted for Fig. 1's ρ_ψ without an equilibrium mapping.

**中文** 第 2 页定义 η_σ=L_nσ/L_Tσ、ε_nσ=2L_nσ/L_B，L_nσ⁻¹=−∂_ρ ln n_σ、L_Tσ⁻¹=−∂_ρ ln T_σ、L_B⁻¹=|∂_ρ ln B|≈1/R。σ=e、i；这里 **ρ=aρ_φ**，ρ_φ=√(Φ/Φ_edge) 使用**环向**磁通，a 是外中平面小半径。Fig. 2(a) 与 Fig. 3 在 ρ_φ=0.4–0.7 平均。没有平衡映射时，不能以 ρ_φ 或有量纲 ρ 直接替代 Fig. 1 的 ρ_ψ。

### Incremental flux and effective pinch | 增量通量与有效箍缩

**EN** On p.3, q_n is the model particle flux per particle, with speed units. Eqs. (1a),(1b) define local incremental diffusivities and a residual pinch term:

**中文** 第 3 页 q_n 是模型粒子通量除以粒子数密度，量纲为速度。Eqs. (1a),(1b) 定义局域增量扩散率及剩余箍缩项：

$$
\widetilde D_{nn}=-\frac{\partial q_n}{\partial(\partial_\rho\ln n_i)},\qquad
\widetilde D_{n\sigma}=-\frac{\partial q_n}{\partial(\partial_\rho\ln T_\sigma)},\quad \sigma=e,i,
\tag{1a}
$$

$$
\widetilde v_n=q_n+\widetilde D_{nn}\partial_\rho\ln n_i
+\widetilde D_{ne}\partial_\rho\ln T_e
+\widetilde D_{ni}\partial_\rho\ln T_i.
\tag{1b}
$$

**EN** With ρ in metres, D̃ has m²/s units and ṽ_n has m/s units. These are derivatives of a declared model flux at a state and a residual defined by identity, not independently measured transport coefficients or a fixed-coefficient law for arbitrarily large gradient changes. Fig. 3 evaluates GLF23 using **experimental profiles as inputs**, unlike the joint profile predictions in Fig. 2. Density-gradient flux is outward, electron thermodiffusion and ṽ_n inward; the ion-temperature contribution is also displayed. Large opposite contributions at low ν_eff weaken as ν_eff rises. A small net flux can therefore coexist with large components.

**中文** ρ 以米计时，D̃ 的单位为 m²/s，ṽ_n 为 m/s。这些是指定模型通量在某状态的导数与按恒等式定义的剩余项，不是独立实测系数，也不是任意大梯度改变下的固定系数定律。Fig. 3 将**实验剖面作为 GLF23 输入**，与 Fig. 2 联合预测剖面不同。密度梯度项向外，电子热扩散与 ṽ_n 向内，图中也列出离子温度项。低 ν_eff 下较大的相反贡献随 ν_eff 增大而减弱，因而净通量小不代表分量都小。

**EN** On p.4 the effective total anomalous pinch combines all non-density-gradient contributions:

**中文** 第 4 页将全部非密度梯度贡献组合为有效反常总箍缩：

$$
\widetilde V=q_n+\widetilde D_{nn}\partial_\rho\ln n_i
=-\widetilde D_{ne}\partial_\rho\ln T_e
-\widetilde D_{ni}\partial_\rho\ln T_i+\widetilde v_n,
$$

$$
-\frac{\widetilde V}{\widetilde D_{nn}}
=-\frac{q_n}{\widetilde D_{nn}}-\partial_\rho\ln n_i
\simeq-\partial_\rho\ln n_i.
\tag{2}
$$

**EN** The last step neglects q_n/D̃_nn relative to the density-gradient term and requires nonzero D̃_nn. This is a small-net-flux approximation, not an assertion that NBI fuelling vanishes. The ratio has inverse-length units under the stated coordinate convention, includes thermodiffusion, and is neither a separately measured pure microscopic pinch nor the Ware velocity. Fig. 4 shows its overall decrease and possible sign change with ν_eff. The authors discuss potentially hollow profiles absent neoclassical effects; Fig. 5 actually compares two representative with/without-Ware simulations, not an experimental removal of Ware.

**中文** 最后一步要求 q_n/D̃_nn 相对密度梯度项足够小，且 D̃_nn 非零。这是小净通量近似，不是宣称 NBI 供粒消失。该比值在上述坐标约定下具有长度倒数量纲，含热扩散，既非独立测得的纯微观箍缩，也不是 Ware 速度。Fig. 4 显示其总体随 ν_eff 下降并可变号。作者讨论无新经典效应时可能形成凹剖面；Fig. 5 实际比较的是两个代表性有／无 Ware 模拟，不是实验移除 Ware。

## 5. Review Approach | FRA 评议方法

**EN** Work performed: direct text extraction and visual inspection of every PDF page; cross-check of the five figures, their legends/axes/captions, all numbered equations, sample conditions and the model-intervention sequence; comparison with the supplied handoff and existing graph.
