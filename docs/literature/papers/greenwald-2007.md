## Why This Study Matters | 为什么选择这项研究？

**EN** Q001 asks what determines density peaking. An empirical model can fit correlated predictors without telling us which will transfer to another device. This study makes that ambiguity experimentally testable by adding a device with different parameter coverage. Its value is the design of a discriminating comparison and the limits it places on an exclusively beam-driven explanation. This page separates the reported evidence from FRA's interpretation and proposed tests. [Original letter, §§1–3](https://doi.org/10.1088/0029-5515/47/9/L03).

**中文** Q001 关心什么决定密度峰化。相关预测变量可能都能拟合同一数据库，却对新装置给出不同预测。本研究通过增加参数覆盖不同的装置，把这种歧义转化为可检验的实验比较。其价值在于有区分力的比较设计，以及对仅由束流供粒解释的约束。本页区分作者报告的证据、FRA 的分析和计划验证。[原刊第 1—3 节](https://doi.org/10.1088/0029-5515/47/9/L03)。

## 3. Research Methods | 研究方法

### Conditions and observations | 条件与观测

**FACT — original-source report / 事实——原文报告：** C-Mod: 51 shots; R=0.67 m, a=0.22 m, B=5.3 T, I=0.65–1.05 MA, volume-average density (1.2–3.8)×10²⁰ m⁻³, ICRF=1.2–5.2 MW, effective collisionality 0.35–8. Quasi-steady ELMy H-modes have nearly equilibrated electron and ion temperatures. / C-Mod 共 51 次放电，装置、密度、功率与碰撞性范围如列；准稳态 ELMy H 模中电子、离子温度接近。**Locator / 位置：L27, §§1–2.**

**中文** 修改平衡后进入更低密度、更高温度区域，既改变碰撞性，也改变相应运行条件。低密度样本报告 H_ITER98y=0.8–1.4、Z_eff=1.4–2.1、辐射占输入功率 0.2–0.5；较高密度样本 Z_eff 约 1.0–1.5。不能把这些变化视为严格“只改碰撞性”的单变量干预。电子与离子温度接近，也不能推出其梯度可独立操纵或完全相同。位置：L27。

**EN** The operating change shifts several quantities together. Matching temperatures does not establish independently varied temperature gradients. Radiation, impurity content, confinement and equilibrium should be retained as covariates in any follow-up design.

**中文** 原文摘要用中心密度与平均密度之比描述峰化，正文与图 2—4 采用 n_e(0.2)/〈n_e〉，另报告 n_e(0.4)/n_e(0.8)。这些都不同于一个指定半径上的局部 R/L_ne。图 1 的横向位置由大半径 R 表达，磁轴 0.69 m、分离面 0.90 m；正文讨论外侧 60% 区域的形状变化与可能由锯齿造成的核心展平，不能据此把中心值和 ρ=0.2 值无条件替换。位置：L26 摘要；L27 图 1 及正文。

**EN** Central/average peaking, off-axis/average peaking, a two-radius density ratio and a pointwise logarithmic gradient are different observables. Core flattening makes the distinction material. Preserve the actual radius and averaging convention in comparisons.

### Source equations and units | 原文方程与单位

**SOURCE EQUATION — collisionality proxy / 原文公式——碰撞性代理量：L26, §1**

$$
\nu_{\mathrm{EFF}}=0.1Z_{\mathrm{EFF}}\frac{\langle n_e\rangle R}{\langle T_e\rangle^2}.
$$

**中文** 〈n_e〉以 10¹⁹ m⁻³ 为数值单位，R 用 m，〈T_e〉用 keV，均值为体积平均。系数包含这些数值单位的约定，不能将 SI 原始数值直接代入。作者指出此前数据将 Z_eff 固定为 2。于是只有在此条件下，该表示才与 Angioni 作者稿的 0.2〈n_e〉R/〈T_e〉² 一致。它也不是任意安全因子、径向位置约定下的 ν_*。Greenwald 分数 f_G=n_e/n_G 使用经验密度限值；正文此处未充分说明该分子密度的全部平均约定，合并时须核对，不能自行替换成体积平均。

**EN** The proxy's prefactor embeds the stated engineering units. The two collisionality conventions coincide only at Z_eff=2. A general ν_* or a differently averaged density must not be substituted. The density averaging in f_G needs an explicit audit before data pooling.

**SOURCE EQUATION — empirical peaking law / 原文公式——经验峰化律：L28, Eq. (1)**

$$
\frac{n_{e2}}{\langle n_e\rangle}
=1.347-0.117\log(\nu_{\mathrm{EFF}})
+1.33\Gamma^{\ast}_{\mathrm{NBI}}-4.03\beta.
$$

**中文** 用途是经验预测全局峰化，不是动理学控制方程。β 是环向 beta 的数值分数，2.5% 应输入 0.025。短文没有完整定义束流项的无量纲化；结合已研读 Angioni 作者稿第 3 节，Γ_NBI^*=RΓ_NBI/(nχ) 是无量纲束源代理量，不能当成有量纲的原始粒子通量。`log` 采用 Angioni 作者稿明确的自然对数 convention；Greenwald 短文本身没有另行声明底数，这一连接依据须保留。式（1）直接引用的是其参考文献 [3] Phys. Plasmas 14, 055905；[4] 是当时已提交的 AUG/JET 联合数据库研究。本页只与已有 Angioni Nuclear Fusion 研读比较同结构、相同舍入系数的式（3），不声称两文的引用链完全相同，也未另行系统研读 [3]。

**EN** Eq. (1) is an empirical predictor. Dimensional conventions and the natural-log convention are linked explicitly to the previously studied manuscript. Its immediate reference is the companion Phys. Plasmas article, while the Nuclear Fusion database paper appears separately in the reference list. Numerical similarity does not establish identical publication provenance.

## 7. Limitations & Open Questions | 局限与开放问题

### Fuelling and microscopic interpretation | 供粒与微观解释

**SOURCE REPORT / 原文报告：** L29, §3 argues for an anomalous pinch using earlier modulated-gas-puff transport results and profile-formation times shorter than the Ware-pinch scale. This is not a new measurement of D or a unique turbulent-flux model in the letter. / L29 第 3 节结合此前调制气体供粒输运研究以及快于 Ware 箍缩时间尺度的剖面形成，提出反常箍缩解释；短文本身不是新的 D 测量或唯一湍流通量模型。

**中文** 这些论据应分层记录：无 NBI 峰化直接约束“只有束流源才能产生峰化”；高边界中性粒子不透明度使直接穿透到核心的供粒较小，却不等于总粒子源严格为零；先前的 D~χ 与快速形成论据支持作者的反常箍缩解释，但前者依赖其参考文献 [9]，此轮没有独立审查该测量。本文没有逐半径源账本、完整瞬态时序或唯一粒子通量闭合。因此**当前不能区分**所有可能的湍流扩散、热扩散、曲率箍缩及其他源与输运耦合。温度接近限制了“必须有很大 T_i/T_e 偏离”的解释，不证明温度梯度机制不存在。

**EN** Separate the no-beam observation from source inventory and from the author's pinch interpretation. Earlier transport evidence is a cited dependency, not independently reproduced here. Opacity does not enforce zero flux. Temperature equality does not eliminate gradient-dependent transport. Competing flux structures remain unresolved.

### Exact reasoning: global peaking does not identify a local gradient | 严格推理：全局峰化不识别局部梯度

**DEFINITION / 定义。** On a fixed radial interval [0,1], let n be positive and C¹, w a fixed integrable nonnegative volume weight, W=∫w>0, and P[n]=n(ρ_c)/(∫wn/W). Let g(ρ_0)=-Λ n′(ρ_0)/n(ρ_0), Λ=R/a>0 fixed (r=aρ), with distinct interior points ρ_c and ρ_0. Assume w is positive on neighbourhoods used below. These are analytical objects for this demonstration, not a claim about the paper's unresolved coordinate convention. / 固定径向区间、正 C¹ 剖面、体积权重、总权重及两个不同内部半径如上；这是说明可识别性的数学对象，不替论文指定坐标。

**STRICT RESULT / 严格结论。** P does not uniquely determine g, even with fixed w and Λ. Choose smooth bumps b₁ near ρ_0 and b₂ away from both points, with b₁(ρ_0)=0, b₁′(ρ_0)≠0, and ∫wb₂≠0. Set h=b₁−(∫wb₁/∫wb₂)b₂. Then h(ρ_c)=h(ρ_0)=∫wh=0, but h′(ρ_0)≠0. For sufficiently small ε, n_ε=n+εh remains positive, P[n_ε]=P[n], while

$$
g_\varepsilon(\rho_0)-g(\rho_0)
=-\frac{\Lambda\varepsilon h'(\rho_0)}{n(\rho_0)}\ne0\quad(\varepsilon\ne0).
$$

**中文——证明含义。** 第一项扰动保留指定半径密度但改变导数；远处补偿项使体积积分不变，且不改变核心观测点。取小扰动保留正性，所以同一全局峰化严格兼容不同局部梯度。无需数值计算即可构造反例。若某模型强制一参数剖面族，可能额外产生映射，但须独立说明该假设。故两文的全局峰化经验关系不能直接升级成局部 equilibrium-response law，更不能识别电子、离子温度梯度的独立偏导响应。

**EN** The construction preserves the measured scalar but changes the derivative. A restricted profile family could supply additional information, but that would be an extra assumption. Peaking regression alone cannot identify local gradient responses.

### Exact reasoning: a steady profile does not uniquely split diffusion and convection | 严格推理：稳态剖面不能唯一分离扩散与对流

**ASSUMPTION — illustrative reduced representation / 假设——说明性约化表示。** On a fixed regular radial chart r, n(r)>0 is C¹, flux Γ is continuous and outward-positive, and D(r)>0 is a freely chosen continuous coefficient. The diffusion–convection representation Γ=−Dn′+Vn is a model assumption, not a microscopic derivation from this letter. Then

$$
V(r)=\frac{\Gamma(r)}{n(r)}+D(r)\frac{n'(r)}{n(r)}
$$

**MATHEMATICAL IDENTITY / 数学恒等式。** Substitution gives the same Γ for every admissible D. Hence knowing both profile and finite flux does not fix D and V separately. / 代入可见任意可容许 D 均对应同一通量；即使剖面和有限通量已知，也不能分别唯一识别 D、V。

For steady flux-surface balance with shell-volume derivative J(r)>0 away from the magnetic axis, a specified net source S satisfies / 对磁轴外 J(r)>0 的稳态磁面粒子平衡，给定净源 S 有

$$
\frac{d}{dr}[J(r)\Gamma(r)]=J(r)S(r),\qquad
J(r)\Gamma(r)=B_0+\int_{r_0}^{r}J(s)S(s)\,ds.
$$

**DERIVED EQUATION / 推导方程。** This follows by integration, with inner boundary flux B₀ prescribed. S has units m⁻³s⁻¹, J m², Γ m⁻²s⁻¹ for this coordinate. Absence of a beam term sets S_NBI=0, not the total integral or B₀ to zero. No amplitude cancellation or zero-total-flux assumption is used. / 由积分得到，内边界通量 B₀ 须给定。无束源只使 S_NBI=0，不使总源积分或边界通量自动为零。未采用幅值消去或零总粒子通量假设。

**Failure boundaries / 失效边界。** The illustrative split needs n>0 and the stated regular chart; at the axis J=0 requires a separate regularity condition. Time dependence adds accumulation to the source balance. A measured transient, additional constitutive law or independent flux information may reduce the ambiguity; it does not follow from an empirical peaking law. / 正性、正则坐标与稳态是明示条件，磁轴 J=0 需另行处理正则性；时变需加入储积项。瞬态或本构关系可减少歧义，但不能由经验峰化律直接获得。

### Transfer and ITER | 转移与 ITER

**中文** 以自然对数、束源代理量为零代入式（1），得到约 1.468；这是代入核对，不是回归复现、独立验证或可靠度区间。1.4—1.6 是作者的外推判断，不是此文明确校准的置信区间。0.15 低于本研究 C-Mod 的最小 0.35；即使 AUG/JET 覆盖较低碰撞性，ITER 的联合几何、源、压强与燃烧条件仍需检验。图 4 不证明任意新装置上的相同误差。

**EN** Arithmetic substitution checks a stated convention only. External-device prediction and a future reactor projection are different evidence levels. Neither provides a microscopic explanation or a calibrated universal uncertainty guarantee.
