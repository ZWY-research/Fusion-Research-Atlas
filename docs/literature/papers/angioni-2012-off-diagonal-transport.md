## Why This Study Matters | 为什么选择这项研究？

**EN** Q001 asks what determines tokamak density peaking. Empirical correlations alone cannot separate diffusion, thermodiffusion, pure convection and finite sources. This survey [@paper-angioni-2012] supplies a conditional kinetic explanation and connects density gradients with toroidal momentum transport. Its value is the combination of mechanisms and discriminating constraints: energy-selective resonances, a nonmonotonic frequency/gradient organisation, and momentum symmetry and inversion limits. These improve the questions asked of models without resolving a unique mechanism or any HL-3 discharge. Anchors: §§2–5, Eqs. (1)–(12), Figs. 1–9.

**中文** Q001 关注托卡马克密度峰化的决定因素。仅有经验相关性不足以分离扩散、热扩散、纯对流与有限源。该综述提供有条件的动理学解释，并联系密度梯度与环向动量输运。价值在于结合机制与判别约束：能量选择共振、非单调的频率—梯度组织规律，以及动量对称性和反演边界。这些认识改善模型应回答的问题，尚未确定唯一机制或解释任何 HL-3 放电。依据：§2–5、Eqs. (1)–(12)、Figs. 1–9。

## 2. Scientific Question | 核心科学问题

**EN — Authors' question.** Why do diagonal diffusion laws fail to determine density and toroidal-rotation profiles? What common kinetic origins and different symmetry constraints govern off-diagonal particle and momentum fluxes, and how do they compare with experiments? Abstract, §§1–2: PDF pp.2–4 / journal pp.1–3.

**中文——作者问题。** 为什么对角扩散律不能充分决定密度与环向旋转剖面？非对角粒子和动量通量有哪些共同动理学来源与不同对称约束，如何与实验比较？依据摘要、§1–2：PDF 2–4 / 期刊 1–3 页。

**EN — FRA reformulation.** Given sources, boundaries, equilibrium and heating, what determines the steady electron density gradient, and why can central ECH either increase or reduce peaking? Can simultaneous particle/momentum constraints distinguish a change in resonant kernels, spectral weights, finite-source balance or residual stress? The density/rotation association is a question about causality, not a law that peaking must reverse rotation.

**中文——FRA 重述。** 给定源、边界、平衡及加热，什么决定稳态电子密度梯度，为什么中心 ECH 既可增强也可减弱峰化？联合粒子与动量约束能否区分共振核、谱权重、有限源平衡及剩余应力变化？密度与旋转的关联提出因果问题，并非“峰化必然导致反转”的定律。

### Particle flux and kinetic assumptions | 粒子通量与动理学假设

**EN** With outward-positive radial flux, Eqs. (1),(5) write the same species' flux as

**中文** 取径向向外为正，Eqs. (1),(5) 对同一物种给出

$$
\frac{\Gamma_n}{n}=-D_n\partial_r\ln n+V_n,
\qquad
\frac{\Gamma_n}{n}=-D_n\partial_r\ln n-D_T\partial_r\ln T
-D_u\frac{R}{v_{th}}\partial_r\Omega_\phi+V_{pn}.
$$

**EN** Here r and R are radial coordinate and major radius, n and T the species density and temperature, Ωφ angular rotation, v_th the chosen thermal speed. D_n,D_T,D_u have diffusivity units; V_n,V_pn are velocities. The electron treatment usually neglects roto-diffusion because of high electron thermal speed; impurities need separate assessment. Eq. (5) decomposes physical contributions, with coefficients depending on gradients through modes and turbulence. It is not a globally linear law. PDF pp.3,5 / journal pp.2,4.

**中文** r、R 为径向坐标与大半径，n、T 为物种密度与温度，Ωφ 为角速度，v_th 为所选热速度。D_n、D_T、D_u 具有扩散率量纲，V_n、V_pn 为速度。电子处理中因热速度大通常忽略旋转扩散，杂质需另行判断。Eq. (5) 分解物理贡献，系数通过模态及湍流依赖梯度，不能作为全局线性律。PDF 3、5 / 期刊 2、4 页。

**EN** Eqs. (3)–(4) motivate the density, energy and velocity-gradient drives from a shifted Maxwellian. Eqs. (6)–(8), §3.1 use local δf gyrokinetics, electrostatic fluctuations and a simplified electron Krook collision term, with ω_k=ω_rk+iγ_k and guiding-centre frequency k_parallel v_parallel+ω_dk. The flux sums over wavenumber and velocity space. Positive ω_r denotes ion-diamagnetic propagation; negative denotes electron-diamagnetic propagation. The E/T_e−3/2 kernel separates lower- and higher-energy thermodiffusive contributions, weighted by resonance and broadening. This is a sign/mechanism explanation, not a complete numerical implementation of Eq. (8), electromagnetic closure or nonlinear saturation prescription. PDF p.5 / journal p.4.

**中文** Eqs. (3)–(4) 从移动麦克斯韦分布引出密度、能量与速度梯度驱动。§3.1、Eqs. (6)–(8) 采用局域 δf 回旋动理学、静电涨落及简化电子 Krook 碰撞项，ω_k=ω_rk+iγ_k，导心频率为 k_parallel v_parallel+ω_dk；通量对波数与速度空间求和。ω_r 正、负分别为离子、电子抗磁传播方向。能量核 E/T_e−3/2 在共振与展宽权重下区分低、高能热扩散贡献。这是符号和机制说明，不是 Eq. (8) 的完整数值实现、电磁闭合或非线性饱和规则。PDF 5 / 期刊 4 页。

### Momentum and cross-channel evidence | 动量与跨通道证据

**EN** Eqs. (2),(9),(10), §§4.1–4.5 distinguish viscous diffusion, Coriolis pinch, momentum convection carried by finite particle flux, and residual stress. Momentum is an odd velocity moment; under the stated lowest-order local symmetry, zero rotation/gradient, up–down symmetry and absent flow shear can give zero averaged momentum flux. Up–down asymmetry, E×B shear and higher-order ρ_* profile variation can break that symmetry. Eqs. (11)–(12), §4.6 connect profile/shear effects with intrinsic rotation, requiring radial momentum balance and boundary/source conditions before predicting the central rotation sign. Anchors: PDF pp.3,9–16 / journal pp.2,8–15; Figs. 4–9.

**中文** Eqs. (2),(9),(10)、§4.1–4.5 区分粘滞扩散、科里奥利箍缩、有限粒子通量携带的动量对流及剩余应力。动量是奇速度矩；在规定的最低阶局域对称条件、零旋转及梯度、上下对称且无流剪切时，平均动量通量可为零。上下不对称、E×B 剪切和高阶 ρ_* 剖面变化可破坏该对称性。Eqs. (11)–(12)、§4.6 联系剖面／剪切效应与本征旋转，预测中心旋转符号前仍须解径向动量平衡并明确边界与源。依据：PDF 3、9–16 / 期刊 2、8–15 页；Figs. 4–9。

**EN** The cross-channel issue in §§4.3–4.6 is that density gradients can modify the Coriolis pinch and higher-order residual stress, while a finite particle flux also carries momentum. Comparing pinch magnitudes or density/rotation correlations therefore cannot by itself isolate one common microscopic cause. Self-consistent parallel mode structure and the terms retained by a transient inversion affect that comparison.

**中文** §4.3–4.6 的跨通道问题在于：密度梯度可改变科里奥利箍缩及高阶剩余应力，而有限粒子通量本身也携带动量。因此仅比较箍缩大小或密度—旋转相关性，不能隔离出唯一共同微观原因。自洽平行模结构以及瞬态反演保留哪些项，均影响这一比较。

A single frequency on the x-axis does not imply a single-mode flux calculation; original reference [51]'s spectral construction remains pending.

w_k=\frac{p_k\hat D_{n,k}}{\sum_jp_j\hat D_{n,j}},
\quad
G_{0,k}=-\frac{\hat D_{T,k}X+R\hat V_k}{\hat D_{n,k}}.
$$

**EN** The nonnegative weights sum to one, so G_0 lies between the mode-wise extrema under these assumptions. Relative spectral weights do not generally cancel. Coefficients and weights may depend on G and X, requiring a self-consistent root; changing the physical amplitude can indirectly change them. For nonzero required turbulent flux, G=G_0+RΓ_req/(n_e A D̄_n) at the same state. This conditional identity does not establish amplitude independence in a real plasma, a universal turbulence law, hysteresis or bifurcation. The diffusivity positivity assumption is essential to the convex bound.

**中文** 非负权重之和为一，因此在这些假设下 G_0 位于单模极值之间；相对谱权重一般不能约去。系数和权重可依赖 G、X，须自洽求根；物理振幅变化也可能间接改变它们。所需湍流通量非零时，同一状态有 G=G_0+RΓ_req/(n_e A D̄_n)。这一条件等式不证明实际等离子体不依赖振幅，也不构成普适湍流规律、滞后或分岔证据；凸界依赖扩散系数为正的假设。

### Independent mathematics C15: finite-source response | 独立数学 C15：有限源响应

**EN** For a magnetic-surface volume V(r), V′=dV/dr, a compatible flux-surface-averaged radial flux and source S_e, assume steady conservation and zero integrated axis flux. Then

**中文** 对磁面体积 V(r)、V′=dV/dr，以及与坐标相容的磁面平均径向通量和源 S_e，假设稳态守恒且磁轴积分通量为零，则

$$
\Gamma_e^{tot}(r)=\frac{1}{V'(r)}\int_0^r V'(r')S_e(r')\,dr'.
$$

**EN** Explicitly separate Γ_tot=Γ_turb+Γ_other and define Γ_req=Γ_source−Γ_other, C=RΓ_req/n_e. Ware or other neoclassical terms must not be counted twice. Nonzero NBI power alone does not quantify Γ_req. At fixed radius and major radius, the local model Γ_turb/n_e=(DG+D_TX)/R+V gives

**中文** 明确分解 Γ_tot=Γ_turb+Γ_other，定义 Γ_req=Γ_source−Γ_other、C=RΓ_req/n_e。Ware 或其他新经典项不可重复计入。NBI 功率非零本身不能确定 Γ_req 大小。固定径向位置与大半径，在局域模型 Γ_turb/n_e=(DG+D_TX)/R+V 下有

$$
F(G,X;\mathbf P)=DG+D_TX+RV-C=0,
$$

$$
\frac{dG}{dX}=-\frac{F_X}{F_G},\qquad
F_X=GD_X+D_T+X(D_T)_X+RV_X-C_X,
\quad F_G=D+GD_G+X(D_T)_G+RV_G-C_G.
$$

**EN** These derivatives require a continuously differentiable closure, fixed other parameters P and F_G≠0; D,D_T,V,C can all depend on G,X. If a differentiable experiment path also changes P, replace F_X by F_X+ΣF_Pj dP_j/dX. Thus D_T<0 alone does not fix the self-consistent gradient response. F_G=0 invalidates this local implicit-function formula; it does not alone prove a transport bifurcation or global dynamical instability. Actual coefficient/source derivatives are unavailable here. The formula organises a future test, not an HL-3 law.

**中文** 求导要求闭合连续可微、其他参数 P 固定且 F_G≠0；D、D_T、V、C 均可依赖 G、X。若可微实验路径同时改变 P，应把分子替为 F_X+ΣF_Pj dP_j/dX。因此仅 D_T<0 不能决定自洽梯度响应。F_G=0 表示局域隐函数公式不适用，不能单独证明输运分岔或全局动力学不稳定。这里没有真实系数及源的响应导数；公式组织未来检验，不是 HL-3 定律。
