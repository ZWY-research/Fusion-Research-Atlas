# Research Insights Template | 科研成果解读模板

[Catalogue / 文献目录](README.md) · [Contribution guide / 贡献指南](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)

**EN** Copy this structure to one file in `docs/literature/papers/`. Replace prompts with English–Chinese content and adjust navigation links for that location (the catalogue becomes `../README.md`). Use an existing DOI record if present, retain source/finding/question IDs, and add the page to relevant domains. This template applies across fusion research, including experimental, theoretical, simulation, diagnostic, materials, and engineering studies. Leave unread or unfinished sections explicitly pending; a completed page layout does not establish a completed scientific review.

**中文** 将本结构复制到 `docs/literature/papers/` 的一个文件中，用中英对应内容替换提示，并按所在位置调整导航链接（目录链接改为 `../README.md`）。优先沿用已有 DOI 记录，保留来源、发现及问题 ID，将页面加入相关领域。本模板适用于聚变领域的实验、理论、模拟、诊断、材料与工程研究。未阅读或未完成栏目明确标为待研究，页面结构完整不等于完成科学评议。

**Default status / 默认状态：Pending review / 待评议。**

## Why This Study Matters | 为什么选择这项研究？

**EN** Identify the FRA question, the authors' specific contribution, the value of the observations/methods/theory, and the directions it opens. Give source anchors and respect the actual reading scope. Avoid unsupported claims of breakthroughs.

**中文** 说明对应的 FRA 科学问题、作者的具体贡献、观测或方法或理论的研究价值，以及打开的后续方向。提供原文依据，尊重实际阅读范围，避免无依据的突破性评价。

## Key Contributions at a Glance | 主要科学贡献概览

- **EN** Summarise 2–4 specific contributions, each with a source locator, conditions and actual status. / **中文** 用 2–4 项概括具体贡献，逐项给出原文位置、条件和实际状态。
- **EN** For unread full text, record only verified selection value and keep contribution assessment pending. / **中文** 未读全文时，只记录已核验的选题价值，贡献评价保留待研究。

## 2. Scientific Question | 核心科学问题

**EN** State the question the paper addresses and why it matters. Define the relevant phenomenon, system, and quantities; distinguish the authors' stated question from FRA's reformulation. Cite the abstract or full-text locator actually consulted.

**中文** 说明论文试图解决的问题及其重要性，定义相关现象、系统和物理量，区分作者原始问题与 FRA 的重新表述，引用实际查阅的摘要或正文位置。

## 3. Research Methods | 研究方法

**EN** Describe the experimental, theoretical, or simulation approach; main inputs; key control variables; model assumptions and orderings; and observables. Include units, boundary/source conditions, data selection, uncertainty, and numerical or diagnostic limitations where relevant. Do not infer independent experimental controls from correlations.

**中文** 说明实验、理论或模拟方法，主要输入、关键控制变量、模型假设与排序，以及观测量。根据研究类型记录单位、边界与源项、数据筛选、不确定性和数值或诊断限制，不将相关性直接解释为独立实验控制。

## 5. Review Approach | FRA 评议方法

**EN** Record work actually done: equation checks, examination of physical conditions, parameter-scan assessment, prior-literature comparison, experiment/model consistency, and competing explanations. Give source locators and analysis artefacts when available. Keep planned tasks in section 10.

**中文** 仅记录已实际开展的方程核对、物理条件检查、参数扫描评价、已有文献比较、实验与模型一致性分析及竞争解释检查，并尽可能给出来源位置与分析材料。计划任务放在第 10 节。

## 6. Scientific Contribution & Novelty | 科学贡献与先进性

**EN** Identify what knowledge, method, or evidence is added relative to specific earlier studies. Separate authors' novelty claims from FRA's evidence-based comparison. If the relevant prior literature or full text has not been studied, write “Not assessed; pending full-text and prior-literature review.”

**中文** 相对具体既有研究，说明新增认识、方法或证据，区分作者的创新性主张与 FRA 基于证据的比较。相关前序文献或全文未研究时，写明“尚未评价；待全文与前序文献评议”。

## 7. Limitations & Open Questions | 局限性与开放问题

**EN** Identify applicability, model assumptions, experimental/diagnostic limits, missing evidence, unresolved competing explanations, and further research difficulties. Distinguish limitations established from the source, limitations of FRA's current reading, and candidate concerns awaiting examination.

**中文** 识别适用条件、模型假设、实验与诊断限制、证据不足、尚未区分的竞争解释以及后续研究困难。区分原文已确认的限制、FRA 当前阅读范围的限制，以及待核查的候选问题。

## 8. Contribution to Fusion Research | 对聚变研究的具体推进

| Level / 层次                                                  | What to substantiate / 需说明的内容                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Direct scientific contribution / 直接科学贡献                 | Which question is better understood, with what evidence and scope? / 哪个问题得到推进，依据与范围是什么？                                                              |
| Potential physical or engineering impact / 潜在物理或工程影响 | How might it improve prediction, control, performance, or engineering, and what further validation is needed? / 可能如何改善预测、控制、性能或工程能力，还需什么验证？ |
| Connection to long-term fusion goals / 长期聚变目标的联系     | Explain the scientific chain linking the result to fusion energy and any missing steps / 说明结果与聚变能源之间的科学联系及尚缺环节                                    |

**EN** Label potential impacts as conditional prospects. Do not present them as demonstrated engineering benefits.

**中文** 潜在影响应标为有条件的前景，不得写成已经验证的工程效果。

## 9. Cross-disciplinary Research Opportunities | 跨学科研究机会

**EN** Translate a specific difficulty into a research task where appropriate: mathematics, nonlinear dynamics, numerical computation, statistical inference, complex systems, experimental design, or another discipline. Define the objects, required inputs, assumptions, physical constraints, and a way to test the answer in the original system. A list of discipline names alone is insufficient.

**中文** 将具体困难转化为可研究任务，可涉及数学、非线性动力学、数值计算、统计推断、复杂系统、实验设计或其他学科。明确研究对象、所需输入、假设、物理约束和返回原系统检验答案的方法，不仅列举学科名称。

**EN** Indexed, completed-review, and independently-verified totals count distinct papers. Promote only the status actually evidenced, identify partial verification explicitly, and update the catalogue and README together. Software checks establish document or data consistency, not scientific validity.

**中文** 收录、完成评议与独立验证均按不同论文去重。只提升实际有证据支持的状态，部分验证须注明范围，并同步更新目录和 README。软件检查验证文档或数据一致性，不代表科学有效性。
